# About "Me" Me

![profile_picture](/assets/images/pages/about-me/author_avatar_200x200.png){: .align-right}
Hello there! My name is Ethan Munday. My passion is video games and throughout my life, I've had so much enjoyment from the immersive worlds and gratifying gameplay across many different genres and themes that games provide. I've always wanted to be involved in games and I happen to be a great mathematician so I decided to become a games programmer.

As a programmer, I like to challenge myself in whatever ways I can. My favourite challenges in code are ones of logic. If there is a complex system that needs building, I will try my hardest to break down the complexity and design a method to create it which is efficient and performant. I push the boundaries of what software is available to me in order to better understand it and often perservere in the worst of times when progress is slow or issues are rampant.

As a colleague, I am active and to the point. I think of myself as an anchor in a team, where I will continue helping others and trudging through work even if the ship is damaged. I believe collaborative efforts often lead to the best results and will always be happy to help other colleagues if asked. Though not a natural born leader, I have often taken up positions of higher responsibility and coordinate with others to ensure any tasks proceed as smoothly as possible.

As a fan of video games, I have experienced a vast range of games. I generally prefer single player, campaign-focused games as an experience however there's always fun to be had with co-op and multiplayer titles. To me, well designed and enjoyable gameplay trumps all. When games flows effortlessly throughout it's areas whilst still delivering the challenges, message and style is when I feel the most immersed and resonates with me hardest. My most played genres would have to be Action, RPG, Adventure and Simulation, though all genres can have standout games that interest me.

As my most recent achievement, I have graduated from the University of Portsmouth's Games Technology course and created an indie games company alongside a group of my peers called [**Circle Back Games**](https://www.circlebackgames.com/). From my experiences, hobbies and education, I have acquired a range of skills that are applicable to different programming roles in game development from working with C++ and C* using Unreal Engine and Unity respectively, to console development on the Nintendo Switch and PlayStation 5. I've currently have 4 years of game development experience and can work with programs like Visual Studio, Github, 3DSMax, Photoshop, Premiere Pro and the Microsoft Office family.
