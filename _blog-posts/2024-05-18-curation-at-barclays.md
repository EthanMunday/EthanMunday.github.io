Recently, we were given the chance through the University of Portsmouth to show off our game at the Barclays Game Frenzy 2024 under their name. It was a great opportunity for us to view the landscape of both smaller UK based companies and other university talent.

The venue was the Barclays Headquaters in London and I have not that much experience in London, let alone in Canary Warf. I may have gotten slightly misdirected when heading to the event but I enjoyed looking around at the pristine buildings and the waterfront restaurants, which made sense considering the large headquaters and well suited people around. The people in the building and hosting the event were pleasant and I think overall Barclays did a great job.

Before going to the event, I did a bit of sleuthing to look at the attendees and what games will be showcased. The website for the event vastly understates the attendees as I was surprised at just how interesting each project at the event was. One particular game that stood out to me was [Paper Trail](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1889740/Paper_Trail/) from Newfangled Games. It was a puzzle game about folding paper to create paths to travel across in a similar aspect to how Monument Valley uses perspective. The game's visual style and mechanics just clicked with me and I will definitely buy that game because of that event (*perhaps Barclays should claim a commision on that sale*). It was very enjoyable getting to socialise with fellow university students and playing their creations. There were many standouts that I do hope see future releases as I would be genuinely interested in buying them. It was also very nice to see people playing our game and giving us feedback, not that we haven't seen this before, however the impact felt higher this time, partly due to many of these participants being new faces and partly due to being in a massive banking tower at a very enjoyable event.

Overall, I had a great time. I would very much enjoy going to similar events and showing our game off more. We do plan on releasing our game publically so it would be nice to get more input and bolster our confidence. The only downside to the trip was the journey home. A multi-hour train ride, completely cramped forcing me to sit basically on the floor in the end of the carrige while hot and bothered with the announcement speakers screeching due to a fault with each use. Still, nothing a nice pizza couldn't solve and I wouldn't have had such a great day without it.
