Personally, I'm very excited because I get the chance to look at newer local indie games as well as get updates from games I have already seen and devs I've interacted with at [Barclays Game Frenzy](/blog-posts/curation-at-barclays/) and [EGX](/blog-posts/curation-at-egx/).
