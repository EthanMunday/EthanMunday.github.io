Well, we did it! We won the Creative Assembly Best Student Game award at the TIGA UK Games Education Awards 2024 (That sure is a mouthful, huh) for our game Curation! This was a surprise to us all but a pleasant surprise to us, nonetheless. It wasn't a clear victory, there were many games on that list, and I had personally played through as many as I could find until I concluded that this was a competitive award (I was also adjacently rooting for [Rider City Rush](https://linktr.ee/VoidFactory)).

We are very proud of this accomplishment. We couldn't have done it without every single member of the team pulling their weight and realising the great opportunity we had to make a truly fun game. This award is also symbolic of our initial determination. Last year, I remember joining a university call where many watched as the team that had the same client and brief as us win the same award. Given the method at which teams were chosen, the clients available, and the luck of the draw to form our unique group, we always had the partial competitive yet cocky motivation that we can do it better than them. The team and I all personally agree that we have done just as good a job if not better at creating something special, however we definitely would not have been as motivated if it wasn’t for their great success, so I thank them along with everyone at the University of Portsmouth who helped us.

Much like how we weren’t the first to win this award, we won’t be the last (probably). I have already heard great things from the team that has come after us and I hope that they do very well. Giving the award to a third simulation game in a brief given by Frontier to a third-year group at the University of Portsmouth might leave a stale taste in the face of other universities however I still want them to do well, partially because that’s what sparked our success, and partially because it would be really, really funny.
