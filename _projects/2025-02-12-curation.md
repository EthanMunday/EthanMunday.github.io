# Create and build the newest museum in town. A place of science and history, wonder and mystery. Set up artefacts, attract customers and build the perfect museum.

{% include feature_row %}

I have worked with a group of talented peers to create a museum sandbox game for a client brief with Frontier Games which we are now furthering development towards commercial release. We are still actively developing and are motivated further by our recent awards win at the TIGA UK Games Education Awards 2024 for Student Game of the Year, as well as our showcases at [Barclays Game Frenzy](/blog-posts/curation-at-barclays/) and [EGX](/blog-posts/curation-at-egx/).
